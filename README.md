# "SuperStore" Profitability Analysis

**Analysis´s target:** Profitability analysis of the "SuperStore" retail chain(Focus - New York City)...
**Where:** Python; Jupiter-Notebook...
**Used tools:** Pandas; SQL; Matplotlib; Time; Scipy; Numpy. Also: Chi2; Cramer´s V; Correlation(Statistics)... 
 
**Findings:** 
1. Discounts of more than 20% in New York result in a loss, mostly;
2. The most profitable time - Autumn-Winter; 
3. The most profitable day is Sunday, and the most unprofitable day is Wednesday;
4. Technology is the most profitable category, and the "Office-Supplies-Products" is the most stable one...

The whole code, visualizations, information about "SuperStore", advices, and statistics are in this file: **"SuperStore_analysis19.ipynb"**
(All information, conclusions, and the most important data are in the very bottom of the code)...
